About Me

I am currently a Research Intern at Hengqin Laboratory. I received my B.Eng. in Intelligence Science and Technology from the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech) in 2026, where I was a member of the Turing Class.

My research has focused on medical image analysis and multimodal intelligence. In medical imaging, CIRSeg and ACME address robust segmentation through test-time adaptation and domain generalization. In multimodal intelligence, WorldGenBench investigates world-knowledge reasoning in image generation, while Point-VLM explores 3D geometric reasoning.

My current research interests lie in the methods and applications of visual intelligence and multimodal agents. I am seeking MPhil/PhD opportunities for Fall 2027.
